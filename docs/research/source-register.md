# Portfolio Research Source Register

- **Research date:** 2026-08-04
- **Applies to:** Issue #2, “Research portfolio purposes, workflows, and compliance constraints”
- **Status:** Foundation research input; not a compliance certification or legal opinion

This register identifies the sources used by the Vitrine foundation research. Source identifiers are cited throughout the accompanying research documents. Temporal scope and authority are explicit because a regulated portfolio profile must be reviewed whenever its governing sources, cohort, school year, or submission system changes.

## Source status vocabulary

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Current | The source appeared current and applicable on the research access date. |
| Current, cohort-specific | Current only for the named graduating class or school year. |
| Current, general | Current general authority, but local application may require policy or counsel review. |
| Future-cohort partial | A confirmed future date or fact is public, but a complete future requirement set is not yet available. |
| Historical research | Used to explain portfolio pedagogy or workflow, not current legal requirements. |
| Restricted detail | The public source confirms that additional controlling detail exists in a restricted system or unpublished template. |
| Review before use | The source must be rechecked before a profile is activated or renewed. |

## Temporal review triggers

A portfolio compliance profile must be reviewed or superseded when any of the following occurs:

1. NJDOE publishes guidance for a new graduating class or school year.
2. The Graduation Assessment Requirements page, ELA guide, mathematics guide, special-population guide, streamlined guide, FAQ, or submission process changes.
3. NJDOE Homeroom releases revised SOA, spreadsheet, field-definition, receipt, or outcome artifacts.
4. N.J.A.C. 6A:8 or N.J.S.A. 18A:7C changes.
5. The statewide assessment platform, cohort definition, pathway rules, cut scores, or submission dates change.
6. New Jersey Treasury or Artemis publishes a revised records schedule or a district records officer classifies the Vitrine output under a different series.
7. FERPA regulations or authoritative federal guidance change in a way that affects access, disclosure, consent, contractors, multi-student records, or de-identification.
8. The DOJ Title II rule, interim rule, WCAG target, compliance date, or applicable exception changes.
9. District publication-consent, acceptable-use, records, accessibility, or identity policies change.
10. A Vitrine profile is copied to another jurisdiction or program.

## Research limitations

- Public web guidance cannot substitute for restricted NJDOE Homeroom templates or local policy.
- The source register records what was available on 2026-08-04; it does not freeze external law or agency guidance.
- The pedagogical sources establish useful workflow patterns but do not impose legal requirements.
- The retention schedule identifies possible record classes, but Vitrine cannot classify a district record without the responsible records officer or policy owner.
- The federal sources provide general constraints. Application to a particular district, disclosure, artifact, or audience may require counsel or an authorized policy decision.
