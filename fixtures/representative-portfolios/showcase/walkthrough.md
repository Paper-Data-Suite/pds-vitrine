# Showcase Portfolio walkthrough

This fixture combines a polished Quillan-shaped individual work with a Concord-shaped Group Artifact. Author, Subject, Group Membership, contribution, and Score target remain separate. The audience package includes a safe contribution statement rather than collaborator display names or internal provenance.

The executable development slice resolves the exact class-qualified synthetic
Portfolio Subject, activates and binds `profile-showcase-rev-001`, discovers the
polished and collaborative sources through Core-backed fixture publication
records, and performs explicit Proposal/Decision/Selection and
Placement/Arrangement workflows.

The collaboration section requires `documented_contributor`. Membership or an
Artifact Subject relationship alone is insufficient. The Concord-shaped
projection keeps the collective Group as Artifact Author, preserves the
Portfolio Subject as member, declared Artifact Subject, and documented
contributor, and retains the represented Group separately. Its scored and
deferred records remain targeted to `concord_group`; neither produces an
individual Candidate or Snapshot Entry.

The collaborative Candidate remains `collaborator_review_required`. Curation
acknowledges that condition, then separately records an audience-safe attribution
Annotation and an exact collaborator-treatment Review. The student-authored
showcase rationale targets the polished and collaborative Selections in that
order. Composition freezes both Annotation revisions and the applicable Review.

`audience-show-external` permits only the five planned audience-visible entries:
the exact polished bytes, exact collaborative bytes, generated attribution,
generated rationale, and generated audience-safe index. The immutable Edition
and `directory_package` Export are digest-verified and replayable. The internal
Manifest, collaborator names/member list, private Quillan data, ScoreForm secure
material, and Portia restricted material are absent. Historical Edition and
Export verification uses sealed Vitrine custody after producer sources are
removed.

This fixture demonstrates curation and audience-presentation boundaries only.
It does not convert a Group Score into an individual Score, infer ownership from
Group Membership, claim live Concord or Quillan integration, authorize a
recipient, issue/deliver a Snapshot, calculate a Grade, or prove proficiency.
