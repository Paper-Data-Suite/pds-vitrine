# Improvement Portfolio walkthrough

The conceptual story is executed by
`scripts/validate_improvement_portfolio.py`. The exact runtime identity,
curation, Snapshot, Export, and source-drift sequence is documented in
`docs/development/improvement-portfolio-vertical-slice.md`.

This fixture follows one synthetic subject across exact class-qualified references. It selects separate Quillan-shaped original-work and student-facing-feedback projections, places baseline and later work in an explicit order, adds a student-authored comparison reflection, and seals a student-facing directory package.

Producer records remain authoritative. The fixture does not claim executable Quillan Core publication, does not calculate improvement, and does not follow a newer source revision after issuance. Private Quillan notes and suppressed Portia material are intentionally absent.
